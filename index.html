D  ·  Worth leaving for the specialist  ·  1 items

Needs measuring before fixing, and the fix carries real regression risk.

—

Ticks and names are stored in the shared worklist and are visible to everyone with this page. The date is stamped for you.

The headline

Three things would actively mislead an incoming specialist. Everything else is ordinary tidying.

The two storefronts are wired together as a UK/US pair, but they don't share product URLs — the UK sells /products/sparkling-rose where the US sells /products/rose. The international linking was built by mirroring the URL path across domains, so on the brand's three flagship wines it points at addresses that redirect or don't exist. Separately, both sites tell search engines they are the same company entity, under a single identifier that carries three different company names. And the homepage of each site has no H1 at all.

Blocking — fix before any SEO work begins

These distort the measurements a specialist would take in week one, so doing them later means redoing the analysis.

SEO-01Theme · gatedBlocking 32 of 131 pages · both sites

International (hreflang) links point at redirects and dead URLs, including all three flagship wines

Each page declares a UK and a US counterpart by taking its own path and swapping the domain. That assumption holds for 99 pages and fails for 32, because the two stores use different handles for the same wine. Google discards a whole hreflang cluster when the return link doesn't resolve, so for the affected pages the two sites currently read as unrelated duplicates competing with each other rather than as regional variants.

SEO-07ThemeHigh Product template

Unrecognised review scores are silently published as 4 stars

Review ratings are converted from words to numbers with a fallback that assigns 4 out of 5 to anything unmatched, so a review whose wording changes — or an empty value — is published to Google as a positive rating that nobody gave. Related: the mpn field is filled with the product's URL handle rather than a manufacturer part number.

sections/main-product-bolle.liquid

What needs doing

Make the fallback omit the review rather than invent a score. Ratings that can't be traced to a real review are a policy risk on top of an accuracy one. Either drop mpn or populate it properly — gtin13 is already correct.

SEO-10ThemeMedium Theme files

The hard-coded UK control was in the header switcher, not the canonical this entry originally named

As first written this entry named two mechanisms and both were wrong. snippets/head-tag.liquid does not write a hard-coded canonical: it emits {{ canonical_url }}, and has done since 20 August 2026. The two stores hold that file byte-identically, and six live URLs across both storefronts serve self-referencing canonicals. layout/theme.liquid did open with assign force_current_site = 'uk' on both stores, but a recursive grep of both themes returned zero readers, so it was inert. The US store carried the identical line while rendering correctly, which is the observation that settles it: had the variable driven anything, US would already have been broken.

The real defect sat in the same file and was never named here. The UK header country switcher was unconditional and always rendered “Switch to US”, while the drawer switcher seventy-one lines below it in the same file was correctly guarded on shop.domain. The US header block was already guarded too. One theme lineage carried both patterns at once, and the correct one sat a short distance from the incorrect one, which is why the fix was a splice of the existing US block rather than new code. On UK the unguarded header happened to produce the right result, so the defect was latent rather than live. The UK theme deployed to a .com store would have offered “Switch to US” to a visitor already on US.

What was done

Fixed 27 August 2026 in three approved pushes. The UK header switcher now carries the guarded block already live on US, byte-identical across both stores. The inert force_current_site line was deleted from both themes, which now return zero hits across 356 UK and 347 US files. Verified by fresh pull and byte diff, and by counting rendered anchors on live pages: UK serves 2 “Switch to US” and 0 “Switch to UK/EU”, US the reverse.
